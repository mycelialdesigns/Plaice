# Slide (and Glide) Bolt-Ons
<img src="/docs/images/slideandglideboltons.webp" alt="A collection of slide and glide bolt-ons" width="256" height="256">

These components allow attaching [slide or glide](/docs/design/SlideInterface.md) connectors of some length `L` in 
[cell units](/docs/design/Units.md) via the [bolt-on interface](/docs/design/BoltOnComponents.md) to [backings](/docs/components/Backings.md) of size `1 CU x L CU`,
effectively allowing slides (or glides) to be mounted arbitrarily to the surface of a Plaice [plate](/docs/components/Plates.md). 

In the picture above, the top row shows glide bolt-ons, and the bottom row shows slide bolt-ons, with protruding slides/glides
to the left and intruding ones to the right.

The positioning of the slide interface is set to match the positioning
of plates which would otherwise be obtained via [crosses and tees](/docs/components/CrossesAndTees.md). They are largely useful
for adding impromptu "dividers" to box-like Plaice constructions without needing to deconstruct and reconstruct
the larger container, as would be required for crosses and tees.
