# Recipe: Shelves

In this recipe, we'll make shelves which are [`18 HU`](/docs/design/Units.md) tall with a `12 CU x 8 CU` surface,
suitable for small desk or kitchen organization tasks (e.g: extra storage for mugs in cabinets). The steps in this
recipe will also cover how to reinforce shelves using M2 nuts and bolts together with [covers](/docs/components/Covers.md),
and how to iterate the construction steps of this recipe to build multi-tier shelf assemblies. 

## Ingredients (Per Tier)
- 1 `8 CU x 8 CU` [Plate](/docs/components/Plates.md)\*
- 1 `4 CU x 8 CU` Plate\*
- 4 `18 HU` tall, `2 CU x 2 CU` Tapered [Beams](/docs/components/Beams.md)\*
### [Plugged](/docs/components/Plugs.md) Variant
- 16 Clipped Plugs
- Optional: 16 M2 Nuts + 16 M2 6mm Bolts
### [Covered](/docs/components/Covers.md) Variant
- 1 `8 CU x 8 CU` Clipped Cover\*
- 1 `4 CU x 8 CU` Clipped Cover\*
- 1 `8 CU x 8 CU` Nubbed Cover\*
- 2 `2 CU x 4 CU` Nubbed Covers\*
- Optional: 28 M2 Nuts + 16 M2 10mm Bolts + 12 M2 6mm Bolts

\*: Any same-size composite of smaller components will also work.

## Step 1
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step1.webp" alt="Step 1 Visual" width="256">

Start by placing down an `8 CU x 8 CU` plate to serve as part of the surface of the shelf.

## Step 2
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step2.webp" alt="Step 2 Visual" width="256">

Slide the `4 CU x 8 CU` plate onto the `8 CU x 8 CU` plate to form a composite `12 CU x 8 CU` plate.

## Step 3
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step3.webp" alt="Step 3 Visual" width="256">

Place one of the `18 HU` beams into the [sockets](/docs/design/Sockets.md) at a corner of
the `12 CU x 8 CU` composite plate, oriented with the body of the beam at the corner socket,
and with the tapers facing toward the center of the plate. 

## Step 4a (Plugged, No Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step4.webp" alt="Step 4a Visual" width="256">

Holding the beam in place with one hand, flip the entire assembly over, and using your other hand,
snap four clipped plugs onto the nubs from the beam, through the sockets. 

## Step 4b (Plugged, No Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step10.webp" alt="Step 4b Visual" width="256">

Repeat the above two steps for the other three beams to finish the shelf. This shelf
as it is suffices to hold a ~`1.5 kg` machinists' hammer (pictured), but its overall
weight capacity is limited by the fact that the legs are attached only by the
clip-and-nub connectors, which will detach once enough force is applied.

To resolve this issue, we can add M2 hardware to reinforce the connection
between the plugs and the legs of the shelf, which we demonstrate in the following steps.

## Step 5 (Plugged, Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step5.webp" alt="Step 5 Visual" width="256">

For each of the clipped plugs mentioned in step 4 a+b, insert a M2 nut into the dedicated
captive nut channel. Make sure that the nuts are pushed as far back as they go with light pressure.

If you had previously done step 4, you can remove the plugs from their sockets by either pulling
off the legs of the shelf with moderate pressure, or by carefully prying the plugs out with a small flathead screwdriver.

## Step 6 (Plugged, Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step6.webp" alt="Step 6 Visual" width="256">

Snap the clipped plugs onto the nubs of the beam, just like in step 4.

## Step 7 (Plugged, Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step7.webp" alt="Step 7 Visual" width="256">

Flip the assembly over. For all but the corner sockets, take 6mm M2 bolts and screw them in to the dedicated
holes on the beam so that they engage with the captive nuts in the clipped plugs on the other side of the socket.
Tighten until you feel moderate resistance, but do not overtighten. 

## Step 8 (Plugged, Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step8.webp" alt="Step 8 Visual" width="256">

Insert 6mm M2 bolts into the holes for the corner sockets using the cut-outs in the beams. For this step, it helps
somewhat to have a magnetic screwdriver or to angle the assembly to assist with bolt seating. Then, take a standard
1.5mm small hex key and tighten the bolts as shown. 

## Step 9 (Plugged, Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step9.webp" alt="Step 9 Visual" width="256">

Repeat the previous three steps for each leg of the shelf.

## Step 10 (Plugged, Bolts)
<img src="/docs/images/shelfconstruction/step10.webp" alt="Step 10 Visual" width="256">

Finish (Plugged + Bolts configuration)
