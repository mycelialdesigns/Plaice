# Recipe: Gridfinity + OpenGrid Desktop Organizer
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/final.webp" alt="Finished Result" width="256">

In this recipe, we'll construct a two-sided desktop organizer for tools and hardware consisting of a
horizontal base of Gridfinity baseplates and a vertical dividing wall with OpenGrid baseplates
on either side of it. In the process, we'll demonstrate how to use bolt-on components (TODO: link)
together with clipped and nubbed backings (TODO: link) to extend Plaice with external components.

## Ingredients

- 4 `2 x 2` Gridfinity Baseplate Bolt-ons (TODO: link) \*
- 8 OpenGrid Baseplate Bolt-ons (TODO: link)
- 2 `4 CU x 8 CU` [Plates](/docs/components/Plates.md) \*
- 1 `8 CU x 8 CU` Plate \*
- 1 `2 CU x 8 CU` Plate \*
- 1 `8 CU` [Protruding Tee](/docs/components/CrossesAndTees.md) \*
- 1 `8 CU x 8 CU` [Nubbed Cover](/docs/components/Covers.md) \*
- 1 `1 CU x 8 CU` Nubbed Cover \*
- 8 `4 CU x 4 CU` Clipped Backings (TODO: Link) \*
- 4 `4 CU x 4 CU` Nubbed Backings \*
- 32 M2 Nuts
- 16 `16 mm` M2 Bolts
- 16 `8 mm` M2 Bolts

\*: Any same-size composite of smaller components will also work.

## Step 1
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step1.webp" alt="Step 1" width="256">

Place the two `4 CU x 8 CU` plates next to each other in the same orientation.

## Step 2
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step2.webp" alt="Step 2" width="256">

Slide the protruding slide between the two plates.

## Step 3
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step3.webp" alt="Step 3" width="256">

Slide the `8 CU x 8 CU` plate onto the protruding slide.

## Step 4
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step4a.webp" alt="Step 4a" width="256"> <img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step4b.webp" alt="Step 4b" width="256">

Flip the entire assembly upside-down and insert the `8 CU x 8 CU` nubbed cover into the
exposed [sockets](/docs/design/Sockets.md). If using a composite of covers, try to
ensure that they span the middle of the assembly rigidly.

## Step 5
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step5.webp" alt="Step 5" width="256">

Insert the `1 CU x 8 CU` nubbed cover into the remaining sockets.

## Step 6
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step6.webp" alt="Step 6" width="256">

Flip the assembly back over and snap four `4 CU x 4 CU` clipped backings onto the bottom
plate (which secures the nubbed covers in place.)

## Step 7
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step7.webp" alt="Step 7" width="256">

Position a Gridfinity baseplate bolt-on over one of the clipped backings, aligning the
holes as best you can.

## Step 8
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step8.webp" alt="Step 8" width="256">

Insert four M2 nuts into the four cornermost captive nut recesses of the Gridfinity
baseplate bolt-on.

## Step 9
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step9.webp" alt="Step 9" width="256">

Hold the Gridfinity baseplate bolt-on in place, and flip the assembly on its side.
At each hole with a nut inserted in step 8, insert and gently tighten an `8 mm` M2 bolt.

## Step 10
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step10.webp" alt="Step 10" width="256">

Repeat the previous steps from step 7 for all Gridfinity baseplates.

## Step 11
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step11a.webp" alt="Step 11a" width="256"> <img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step11b.webp" alt="Step 11b" width="256">

Take one `4 CU x 4 CU` clipped backing and one nubbed backing and snap them together on
either side of the vertically-oriented plate, leaving at least `2 CU` of the plate at the
bottom for clearance between Gridfinity and OpenGrid components.

## Step 12
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step12.webp" alt="Step 12" width="256">

Take an OpenGrid baseplate bolt-on and hold it in alignment with the holes of the backings
from the previous step (either side will work).

## Step 13
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step13a.webp" alt="Step 13a" width="256"> <img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step13b.webp" alt="Step 13b" width="256">


With your free hand, insert four `16 mm` M2 bolts into the corner holes of the OpenGrid
baseplate bolt-on. So long as you keep the assembly upright, you will no longer need
to hold onto the OpenGrid baseplate like you did in step 12.

Turn the assembly around so that you can see the bolts protruding through.

## Step 14
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step14.webp" alt="Step 14" width="256">

Snap four M2 nuts into another OpenGrid baseplate bolt-on and gently slide it on to the exposed
ends of the bolts from the previous step. Hold the two baseplates on either side together with
one of your hands.

## Step 15
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step15a.webp" alt="Step 15a" width="256"> <img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step15b.webp" alt="Step 15b" width="256">


Gently tighten the four bolts you inserted in step 13. Once completed, you should be able
to free both of your hands.

## Step 16
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step16.webp" alt="Step 16" width="256">

Repeat the previous steps from step 11 for another pair of OpenGrid baseplates.

## Step 17
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step17.webp" alt="Step 17" width="256">

Slide the `2 CU x 8 CU` plate on top of the vertically-oriented `8 CU x 8 CU` plate.

## Step 18
<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/step18.webp" alt="Step 18" width="256">

Repeat steps 11-15 to secure the remaining OpenGrid baseplates.

<img src="/docs/images/gridfinityopengridconstruction/final.webp" alt="Finished Result" width="256">
Finish
