# Recipe: Spice Organizer
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/final.webp" alt="Finished Result" width="256">

In this recipe, we'll make a modular spice organizer suited for small-to-mid-size spice containers.
In the process, we'll demonstrate how to orient slides in grid-structured assemblies
involving [crosses and tees](/docs/components/CrossesAndTees.md).

## Ingredients (`W` x `H` Array)

- `2 * W * H + W + H` Copies of `2 CU x 4 CU` [Plates](/docs/components/Plates.md)

- `(W - 1) * (H - 1)` slide-crosses

- `W + H - 2` protruding slide-tees

- `W + H - 2` intruding slide-tees

- 4 [Corners](/docs/components/Corners.md)

## Step 1
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step1.webp" alt="Step 1 Visual" width="256">

Lay down all the plates which make up the base of the organizer as shown. Ensure that all
protruding slides are pointing in the same direction.

## Step 2
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step2.webp" alt="Step 2 Visual" width="256">

In between each adjacent pair of places, slide on some of the tees. Make sure to
alternate between intruding and protruding tees, this will be important later.
Don't worry about getting the alignment perfect, this can always be corrected later.

## Step 3
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step3.webp" alt="Step 3 Visual" width="256">

Slide the corners onto the base.

## Step 4
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step4.webp" alt="Step 4 Visual" width="256">

For every upward-facing slide (protruding or intruding), slide a plate on it.

## Step 5
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step5.webp" alt="Step 5 Visual" width="256">

For all upward-facing slides which are not on the ends, slide a cross onto it.
Ensure that the orientation of the crosses is such that for every partially-completed
top "cell" (which would accommodate a spice container), the orientation of protruding
slides is consistent (clockwise or counterclockwise) about the loop around the edges of the cell
(see picture). 

## Step 6
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step6.webp" alt="Step 6 Visual" width="256">

Slide protruding and intruding tees onto the edges, making sure to follow the orientation
constraint mentioned in the previous step.

## Step 7
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/step7.webp" alt="Step 7 Visual" width="256">

Between every adjacent pair of crosses, tees, or a pairing of a cross and a tee, slide
a plate between them. Like in step 1, their protrusions should all face in the same
direction, but one which is the opposite of the previous tier.

## Step 8
<img src="/docs/images/spiceorganizerconstruction/final.webp" alt="Step 8 Visual" width="256">

Iterate the previous steps from step 4, with the top tier instead substituting corners
in place of tees and tees in place of crosses.
